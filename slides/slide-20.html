Sources: Arborist · FLASH · REMAC

Related ideas 1, 2, and 4 from Slide 19. Fifteen-second sequence with one main idea per paper, followed by a complete static view. Arborist expands FMT* trees in parallel; speculative read-only neighbor queries run ahead of validated, in-order updates. It does not represent multiple robots. FLASH drafts actions with a lightweight model and verifies them in parallel with the main Action Expert, retaining the accepted prefix. Full inference is available when no prefix survives or during fine adjustment; the fallback branch indicates an option, not a response to every rejection. REMAC masks prefix loss during training to learn corrective suffix actions. At runtime, prefix-preserved sampling keeps the executed overlap and predicts the suffix. Training masks never delete live actions. Block counts are schematic. These are independent related systems, not integrations evaluated in ReCA. Printing and reduced-motion show the complete view.
