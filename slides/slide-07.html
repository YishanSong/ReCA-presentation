Dual Memory Structure

LONG-TERM PERSISTENT MENORY

Expand the hierarchical graph as new areas are explored; serialize it into structured text for the LLM planner.

SHORT-TERM / DYNAMIC

Replace least recently used data with recent information; prioritize task-relevant details to prevent context dilution.

TAKEAWAY

Minimize redundant exploration.
