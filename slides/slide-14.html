Why Is A* Hard to Accelerate?

RESEARCH QUESTION

Why is Bob’s A* search costly on general-purpose CPUs and GPUs?

COMPUTATIONAL CHALLENGES

Sequential Dependencies

Each expansion depends on the updated search state.

Irregular Control and Memory Access

Frequent heap updates and data-dependent branching.

KEY FINDING

CPU: repeated heap and memory operations.

GPU: dependent iterations limit parallelism.
